Candlestick Patterns

A Bullish Engulfing pattern forms when a large bullish candle completely engulfs the previous bearish candle.

Risk Management

Never risk more than 1% of account equity per trade. Always use a stop loss. Risk reward should be at least 1:2.

Trend Following Strategy

Trade only in the direction of the higher-timeframe trend. Avoid trading during low volatility.

Bear Flag Pattern

A Bear Flag is a continuation chart pattern. Price forms a downward impulse and then a small upward channel.
